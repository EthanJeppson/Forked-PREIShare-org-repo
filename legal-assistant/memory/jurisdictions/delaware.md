## Blackletter the firm will state (high altitude)

- Choice of Delaware law in a commercial contract is generally respected when the contract is between sophisticated parties.
- Entity-governance disputes for Delaware entities are commonly steered to the Court of Chancery when the parties so provide.
- On **sandbagging**, Harbor & Quill’s *negotiation default* on Delaware SPAs is pro-sandbagging (`HQ-K-SANDBAG-DE`). That is a firm precedent, not a holding.

## Splits / nuance (`status: split`)

- Anti-sandbagging clauses are a negotiated term; do not tell the attorney “Delaware prohibits anti-sandbagging.”
- Advancement and indemnification for officers are statute- and charter-dependent; do not generalize from one target.
